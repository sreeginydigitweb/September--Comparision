# closure/

Evidence that the project, or a defined stage of it, is finished.

## What belongs here

- Stage completion records, with what was delivered and what was explicitly excluded
- Final sign-off notes and acceptance against the agreed scope
- Residual risks accepted at close

## What does not belong here

- Work still in progress
- Anything that is a handover rather than an ending — that belongs in `handover/`
- Raw validation output — that belongs in `validation/`
